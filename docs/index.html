💼 Job Dashboard

Charts :
1.Donut Chart: Displays the total number of job-related emails received in the inbox.
2. Bar Chart: Shows the number of emails received over the past 10 days.

Best features in Tables :
➤ AI-Generated Summary: A brief overview of the company, generated automatically using AI.
➤ AI-Recommended Next Steps: Suggests the best subsequent action based on the email content.

AI Based Recommendation System :
The recommendation system operates in two modes:
1.Generic Mode: Analyzes all received job opportunities and identifies the best overall opportunity based on general job-related criteria.
2.Personalized Mode : Analyzes the received job opportunities against the user's profile and recommends the opportunity that best matches their skills, experience, and career profile.

Testing section :
A sandbox email account, pre-connected to the project, is provided, allowing you to instantly test the dashboard by sending job-related emails from a recruiter’s perspective.

📄 Resume Designer — Profile

My Profile :
Paste your resume once — the AI chops it into pieces and indexes the profile.
➤ Append & Re-index: Adds new text (a project documentation, skill or certification) to the existing profile without rewriting the entire profile.

Job Description :
Paste the full JD you want to target — then an AI Model retrieves the most relevant chunks of your profile and writes a structured, ATS-friendly resume for this exact job.

✨ Tailored Resume — what you get

Generated resume :
1. Match Score: Shows how strongly your profile fits the JD, as a percentage.
2. Grounded Content: Every summary, skill and bullet comes from your real evidence — the editor polishes words but never changes facts.
3. Watch-outs: Highlights genuine skill gaps and areas for improvement. These are not included in the resume, as they are not part of the user’s actual profile.
4. The generated resume will be available in three formats: downloadable text, Word document, and PDF.

Main Use case :
Regularly updating the profile with precise and accurate information about projects, skills, and certifications enables the system to instantly generate a more accurate, effective and Job-ready resume.

🧪 RAG Eval Lab

The quality gate: retrieval recall, faithfulness, citation accuracy, unsupported-claim rate, latency and cost per run is provided for reference. It proves every resume claim is backed by retrieved evidence.
